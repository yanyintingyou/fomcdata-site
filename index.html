Public Data Source Index公开数据源索引

Statistical databases, official releases, and literature for research on the Federal Open Market Committee.与联邦公开市场委员会研究相关的统计数据库、官方发布与文献来源。

01Federal Reserve美联储

  • Federal Reserve Board美联储理事会federalreserve.gov

    Monetary policy framework, policy statements, and research.货币政策框架、政策声明与研究报告

  • FOMC Meeting MaterialsFOMC 会议材料federalreserve.gov

    Statements, minutes, and transcripts from past meetings.历次会议的声明、纪要与记录文件

  • FREDstlouisfed.org

    Economic time series for the United States and the world, over 800,000 indicators.美国及国际经济时间序列，覆盖八十余万条指标

  • H.15 Selected Interest RatesH.15 利率federalreserve.gov

    Treasury yields, the federal funds rate, and commercial paper rates.国债收益率、联邦基金利率与商业票据利率

  • Z.1 Financial AccountsZ.1 金融账户federalreserve.gov

    Balance sheets and flow of funds for every sector of the U.S. economy.美国各部门资产负债表与资金流量

  • Beige Book褐皮书federalreserve.gov

    Business conditions across the twelve Federal Reserve districts, published two weeks before each meeting.十二个联储辖区的景气调查，会前两周发布

  • FRED-MD / FRED-QDstlouisfed.org

    Monthly and quarterly macro databases for large-scale factor extraction.月度与季度宏观数据库，适合大规模因子提取

02International Organizations国际组织

  • BIS Statisticsbis.org

    Cross-border bank claims, effective exchange rates, credit gaps, and property prices.跨境银行债权、有效汇率、信贷缺口与房地产价格

  • IMF Dataimf.org

    International financial statistics, balance of payments, reserve composition, and official foreign exchange reserves by currency.国际金融统计、国际收支、储备构成与官方外汇储备币种

  • OECD Dataoecd.org

    Economic, financial, and social indicators for member countries.成员国经济、金融与社会指标

  • World Bank Open Data世界银行公开数据worldbank.org

    Global development indicators and country-level macro data.全球发展指标与国别宏观数据

  • ECB Data Portal欧洲央行数据门户ecb.europa.eu

    Monetary, financial, and supervisory statistics for the euro area.欧元区货币、金融与监管统计

03Fiscal and Cross-Border Capital财政与跨境资本

  • Treasury TIC美国财政部 TICtreasury.gov

  • Treasury Fiscal Data财政部财政数据treasury.gov

    Federal receipts, outlays, debt, and Treasury auction details.联邦财政收支、债务与国债发行明细

  • Treasury International财政部国际事务treasury.gov

    Exchange rate policy reports and international economic affairs documents.汇率政策报告与国际经济事务文件
