Datasets数据集

Compiled datasets live in their own repositories, versioned by release and archived with a DOI. Each carries a variable dictionary, the construction code, and a source list, so a result can be rebuilt from the inputs.编制数据集单独成库，按版本发布并归档取得 DOI。每份附变量字典、构造代码与来源清单，结果可以从输入重跑。
