Update cycle更新周期

A GitHub Actions job runs once a day at 23:17 UTC, after the US close. It fetches each source, merges the new window into the stored history, commits the data file, and deploys the Worker. GitHub starts scheduled jobs a few minutes to half an hour late.GitHub Actions 每天 23:17 UTC 跑一次，时点在美股收盘之后。任务抓取各来源，把新窗口合并进已存历史，提交数据文件，再部署 Worker。GitHub 的定时任务通常延后几分钟到半小时启动。

Window and sampling窗口与采样

Each run requests roughly the last 400 days. Older points stay as stored. Points older than 90 days are thinned to one per week; the last 90 days keep every observation. One year adds about 52 points per series.每次只请求最近约 400 天，更早的点沿用已有数据。90 天以前按周采样，最近 90 天保留全部观测。每个序列每年新增约 52 个点。

Failure handling失败处理

When a fetch fails, the stored points for that series are kept, the series is flagged stale, and the error text is written to the data file. The tile shows a dot and the chart prints the cutoff date. Other series update as usual.某个序列抓取失败时，保留它已有的点，标记为过期，并把错误原文写进数据文件。指标格子出现小圆点，图表下方注明截止日期。其余序列照常更新。

Definitions定义

  • EFFR is the effective federal funds rate published by the Federal Reserve, series DFF. It is an overnight rate, not the target range.联邦基金有效利率取自美联储 DFF 序列，是隔夜成交利率，不是目标区间。
  • CPI year over year divides the CPIAUCSL index by the same month a year earlier. Months whose base is missing are left out.CPI 同比用 CPIAUCSL 指数除以上年同月。缺基数的月份不出点。
  • Gold and WTI are front-month futures on COMEX and NYMEX, continuous series GC=F and CL=F. They roll, so small gaps at contract changes are normal.黄金与 WTI是 COMEX 与 NYMEX 的期货连续合约 GC=F、CL=F。合约换月处的小跳空属于正常。
  • VIX is the CBOE volatility index as carried by Yahoo Finance.VIX为 CBOE 波动率指数，经 Yahoo Finance 获取。
  • Recessions are NBER dates as encoded in FRED series USREC.衰退区间采用 NBER 判定，取自 FRED 的 USREC 序列。
