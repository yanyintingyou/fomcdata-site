About关于

A small index of public data sources for FOMC research, plus a daily snapshot of seven series drawn from those sources.一个 FOMC 研究用的公开数据源索引，另附七个序列的每日快照。

What it is定位

An index of public data sources for research on the Federal Open Market Committee, with a daily snapshot of policy rates, Treasury yields, inflation, employment, credit conditions, equities, gold, crude and volatility. Thirty-five series, drawn from FRED and Yahoo Finance, refreshed once a day at 23:17 UTC.面向美联储研究的数据源索引，附一份政策利率、美债收益率、通胀、就业、信用条件、股指、黄金、原油与波动率的每日快照。三十五个序列取自 FRED 与 Yahoo Finance，每天 23:17 UTC 刷新一次。
