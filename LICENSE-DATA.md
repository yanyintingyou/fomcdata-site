# Data terms

`LICENSE` (MIT) covers the site code: `public/index.html`, `public/style.css`,
`public/404.html`, `scripts/`, `.github/workflows/`, `wrangler.toml`.

Data files carry the terms of their sources.

## public/data/market.json

| Series | Source | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| Effective fed funds rate (`DFF`) | FRED, Federal Reserve Board | US government series, public domain |
| CPI, year over year (`CPIAUCSL`) | FRED, Bureau of Labor Statistics | US government series, public domain |
| Unemployment rate (`UNRATE`) | FRED, Bureau of Labor Statistics | US government series, public domain |
| S&P 500 (`^GSPC`) | Yahoo Finance | Index values © S&P Dow Jones Indices |
| Gold, front month (`GC=F`) | Yahoo Finance | COMEX quotes |
| WTI crude, front month (`CL=F`) | Yahoo Finance | NYMEX quotes |
| VIX (`^VIX`) | Yahoo Finance | Index values © CBOE |

The market series belong to the publishers named above, and their terms govern
reuse of those values.

## Research datasets

Datasets built for papers are released as separate repositories, each with its
